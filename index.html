Manifesto

Black Izba is folklore printed like an old curse: worn black, bone ink, rough lines, dead legends and stubborn memory.

We draw from Slavic folk horror, occult engraving, black metal graphics, darkwave melancholy and the strange comfort of old village nightmares.

The house stands on chicken legs, the forest remembers names, and the old spirits still ask for tribute.
